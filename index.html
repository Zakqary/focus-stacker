    Tips for good stacks
    • Keep the camera and subject still; change only the focus between shots.
    • Take enough photos that every part of the subject is sharp in at least one of them, usually 5 to 20.
    • Keep exposure and white balance the same for every shot if your camera allows it.
    • The photos must be in focus order. Sorting by file name usually gets this right; if the result looks odd, try “Order by → File date”.
    • Pyramid is best for hairs, bristles and fine texture. Depth map keeps colours cleaner. You can make both and keep whichever looks better.
    • On a computer you can drag photos, or several folders at once (one stack per folder), onto the page.
